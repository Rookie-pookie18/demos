Immigrate · Express Entry

Express Entryruns on points.

Candidates for the Federal Skilled Worker, Federal Skilled Trades and Canadian Experience Class programs are ranked in one pool by the Comprehensive Ranking System. In each draw, IRCC invites the highest-ranked profiles.

A · Core human capital · up to 500

Who you are,on paper.

Age, education, official-language ability and Canadian work experience. Up to 460 if a spouse or partner is coming with you.

B · Spouse or partner · up to 40

Who comeswith you.

Your partner’s education, language results and Canadian work experience.

C · Skill transferability · up to 100

How your strengthscombine.

Strong language results with a degree, or foreign work experience alongside Canadian experience, add up to more than either alone.

D · Additional points · up to 600

The factors thatmove a profile.

A provincial nomination adds 600 on its own. French ability, study in Canada and a brother or sister living here also count.

Before you enter the pool

We model your scorebefore you file.

Cut-off scores change with every draw, so we don’t quote one. We show you which factors you can still improve.

Questions

What people ask first

Answers adapted from our current site. RCIC to review Some details may be out of date and must be checked before launch.

Who can be included in a permanent residence application?

Usually the main applicant, their spouse, common-law or conjugal partner, and dependent children. Older children may qualify in some circumstances. Age limits and definitions are set by IRCC and change over time.

Where is the application submitted?

Most economic applications are now filed online with IRCC. Depending on the program, parts of the file may be handled by an office inside or outside Canada.

Do I need to travel to Canada during the process?

Generally no. Some programs value familiarity with the province or community you plan to live in.

Will I have to attend an interview?

Most applicants are not interviewed, but IRCC can request an interview with the applicant and, sometimes, family members.

How does permanent residence affect my taxes?

Canada taxes on residency, not citizenship. Once you become a resident for tax purposes, you are generally taxed on your worldwide income. Speak to a tax professional about your situation.
