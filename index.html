Two ways to build
the same good site.

Every demo below is a real, working website — not a picture of one. Open them on your phone. They come in two tiers so you can pick the one that fits your budget rather than trimming a single quote down until it stops working.

Standard Tier one

A conventional site done properly. Loads instantly on a cheap phone on patchy data, which is how most of your customers will see it.

  • 3–4 pages: home, about, products or services, contact
  • Clean layout, real typography, subtle scroll reveals
  • Enquiry form and WhatsApp click-through
  • Search-engine basics: titles, descriptions, sitemap
  • Mobile-first, no heavy graphics, near-instant load

Premium Tier two

A homepage that behaves like a short film as you scroll, with one signature effect built specifically for your industry — plus everything in Standard.

  • Everything in Standard, then the full site around it
  • One custom 3D or scroll-driven centrepiece, built in code
  • A page per product or service, written for search
  • Motion tuned for phones, with a still fallback on old devices
  • The version people screenshot and send to someone else
