02 · The idea

Every summer the rose fields around Kannauj are picked at dawn for attar. We take the petals the distillers leave behind, dry them in the shade, and fold them into the best leaf we can buy. That's the whole recipe.

05 · The ritual

Four minutes.No shortcuts.

WarmThe pot

Swirl hot water round the pot first. Cold china steals the heat.

2.5 gPer cup

A heaped teaspoon. Petals are light, so heap it.

90°COff the boil

Let the kettle rest a minute. Greens want three.

3 minThen pour it all

Nothing left sitting on the leaf. Steep again for a softer cup.

06 · Where it comes from

Two cities,one cup.

Kannauj has distilled rose into attar for centuries. Kolkata has sold India's tea to the world for almost as long. We sit between the two.
