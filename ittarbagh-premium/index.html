05 / 08 · The ritual

Four minutes,done properly.

Rose is delicate. Boil it hard or steep it too long and all you taste is tannin. Here's how we brew at the blending table.

i.
Warm

Rinse the pot

Swirl hot water in the pot or cup first, then tip it out. Cold china steals heat.

ii.
2.5 g

A heaped teaspoon

Per 200 ml cup. Petals are light, so a heaped spoon, not a level one.

iii.
90°C

Just off the boil

Let the kettle rest for a minute. Greens want 80°C, so rest it for three.

iv.
3 min

Then pour it all

Leave nothing sitting on the leaf. A second steep is softer and more floral.
